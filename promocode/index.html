Redeem your promo code

Thanks for testing Polypulser! You redeem your promo code in the App Store or Google Play. Pick your phone and follow the steps.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the App Store and tap your profile picture at the top right.

    Don’t see it? Tap Today at the bottom first.

  2. Tap Redeem Code.

    On older iOS versions it’s a row called Redeem Gift Card or Code.

  3. If the camera opens, choose to enter the code manually. Type your code and tap Redeem.

    XXXX XXXXRedeem

Android

  1. Open Google Play and tap your profile icon at the top right.

  2. Tap Payments & subscriptions.

  3. Tap Redeem code.

  4. Type your code and tap Redeem.

    XXXX XXXXRedeem

That’s it. The store confirms the code, and then you can open Polypulser.

Still stuck?

First check the code for typos. If it still doesn’t work, send a screenshot of the message and your phone model to:

support-polypulser@forpilot.ch
