Common questions

The metronome keeps playing when I lock my iPhone or switch apps.

That is intended so you can play along with other apps. Return to Polypulser and press Stop to end it.

There is no sound.

Check the volume. The metronome ignores the silent switch, so use the volume buttons. Also check that the beats are not set to Silent. A beat with a rest rhythm is silent by design.

My music stops when I start the metronome.

Open Settings in Polypulser and switch on Play Over Other Audio.

How do I put different numbers of beats on different lines?

Touch and hold a beat's circle until it lifts, then drag it to another line. Drag it above the top line or below the bottom line to start a new line. The small circular arrow in the corner of the beat display restores the automatic layout.

How do I give a beat its own rhythm?

Tap the notes under the beat and choose a pattern. To use the same rhythm everywhere, tap Use for All Beats or the Rhythm tile next to the Beats stepper.

How do I move my setlists to another device?

On the Setlist screen, open the … menu and choose Share Setlist. Send the file by AirDrop, Messages or Mail. On the other device, open the file and choose Polypulser, or use Import Setlist.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
