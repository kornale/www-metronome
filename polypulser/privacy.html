Privacy Policy

Polypulser for iPhone. Last updated: 21 September 2026.

The short version

Polypulser does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves your settings, your last Basic and Poly setups, and the songs and setlists you create. This information is kept only in the app's storage on your iPhone. We cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It does not connect to the internet or to any server.
  • It contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or third-party software that collects data.
  • It does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Sharing setlists

If you choose Share Setlist, the app creates a file with your songs and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files) is decided by you. Importing a setlist reads a file you select. Nothing is sent or received unless you do it.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or your computer, the app's data may be included in that backup. This is handled by Apple under your own settings and is not accessible to us.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
